    Advanced AI Engineer · CATL

    Aobo Yang

    I build physics-aware AI systems for engineering design—from battery materials and manufacturing to aircraft and satellite optimization.

    I am based in Hong Kong and work at CATL, focusing on AI4Science for materials discovery in EV batteries and energy-storage systems, alongside manufacturing-process optimization.

    I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from HKUST, advised by Prof. Rhea Liem. I was also a visiting Ph.D. researcher at the EPFL Computer Vision Lab with Prof. Pascal Fua, and previously worked on multidisciplinary design optimization at UC San Diego.
